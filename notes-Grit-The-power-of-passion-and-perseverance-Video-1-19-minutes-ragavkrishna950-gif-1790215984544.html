IQ means intelligence or the ability to understand and solve problems. IQ can help a person learn, but it is not the only factor for success. Passion means having strong interest and enthusiasm toward something. A passionate person is willing to learn, practise and work hard. Passion helps people continue even when they face failures and difficulties. Many successful people became good at their work through interest, practice and determination. A person with average intelligence can achieve great things if they are passionate and hardworking. Passion makes learning more interesting and meaningful. Hard work, dedication, creativity and persistence are also important for success. Therefore, instead of worrying only about IQ, people should discover their passion and develop their skills. Main idea: IQ may help us, but passion, continuous effort and dedication can play a major role in achieving our goals.
